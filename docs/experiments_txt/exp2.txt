Title
Apply various text preprocessing techniques for any given text: Tokenization, Filtration and Script Validation
Case Study: Resume Keyword Extraction
Theory
Text preprocessing is one of the fundamental steps in Natural Language Processing (NLP). Real world textual data is usually unstructured and
contains unnecessary information such as punctuation marks, numbers, special symbols, and inconsistent formatting. Before applying machine
learning or NLP techniques, it is important to clean and organize the text so that it becomes suitable for analysis.
In Resume Keyword Extraction, textual information is extracted from resumes to identify important skills, qualifications, technologies, and job-
related keywords. The quality of the extracted text directly affects the performance of keyword extraction systems. Therefore, text preprocessing
plays a significant role in improving the accuracy and efficiency of NLP applications.
In this experiment, three important text preprocessing techniques are performed on a Resume Dataset.
1. Tokenization
Tokenization is the process of splitting textual data into smaller meaningful units called tokens. Tokens can be words, characters, or sentences.
Word tokenization is one of the most commonly used techniques in NLP because it converts a sentence into individual words that can be easily
analyzed by machine learning algorithms.
Tokenization helps in understanding the structure of the text and forms the basis for various NLP tasks such as keyword extraction, information
retrieval, text classification, and language modeling.
Example
Input:
The candidate has experience in Python, Machine Learning, and SQL.
Output:
['The', 'candidate', 'has', 'experience', 'in', 'Python', ',', 'Machine', 'Learning', ',', 'and', 'S
QL', '.']
2. Filtration
Filtration is the process of removing unnecessary or irrelevant information from textual data. Raw text often contains punctuation marks, numbers,
extra spaces, special symbols, and unwanted characters that do not contribute significantly to text analysis.
Filtering the text helps in reducing noise and improving the overall quality of the dataset. Clean and filtered text allows NLP models to focus only
on meaningful information present in resumes.
Filtration may include the removal of:
Punctuation marks
Numbers
Special characters
Extra white spaces
Unwanted symbols
Example
Input:
Experience: Python, SQL, C++, and Machine Learning (3+ years).
Output:
Experience Python SQL C and Machine Learning years
3. Script Validation
Script validation is the process of verifying whether the textual data belongs to a particular writing system or language script. In this experiment,
script validation is performed to ensure that the resume text contains only valid English alphabetic characters after filtration.

Improves the quality and consistency of textual data.
Removes unnecessary information from the dataset.
Increases the performance of NLP models.
Reduces computational complexity.
Helps in preparing structured data for machine learning algorithms.
Improves the accuracy of resume keyword extraction.
Enables efficient text analysis and feature extraction.
