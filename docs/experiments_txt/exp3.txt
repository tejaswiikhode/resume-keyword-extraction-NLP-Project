Theory
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables computers to understand, interpret, and process
human language. Before performing tasks such as text classification, keyword extraction, sentiment analysis, or machine learning, textual data
must be preprocessed. Text preprocessing improves the quality of text by removing unnecessary information and converting it into a structured
format suitable for analysis.
In this experiment, the preprocessing techniques Stop Word Removal, Lemmatization, and Stemming are applied to resume text for
keyword extraction.
Stop Word Removal
Stop words are commonly occurring words in a language that carry little or no meaningful information for text analysis. Examples include "the,"
"is," "and," "with," "in," and "of." Removing stop words reduces the size of the text and helps focus on important words that contribute to the
meaning of the document.
Example
Original Sentence:
Experienced Python developer with expertise in machine learning and data analysis.
After Stop Word Removal:
Experienced Python developer expertise machine learning data analysis.
Lemmatization
Lemmatization is the process of converting words into their base or dictionary form, known as the lemma. It considers the meaning and
grammatical context of a word, producing valid dictionary words. This technique helps normalize text while preserving its meaning.
Examples
Original Word Lemmatized Word
studies study
cars car
running run
developers developer
Stemming
Stemming is the process of reducing words to their root form by removing prefixes and suffixes using predefined rules. Unlike lemmatization,
stemming does not consider the meaning of words and may produce non-dictionary words. However, it is faster and commonly used in
information retrieval systems.
Examples
Original Word Stemmed Word
learning learn
developer develop
experienced experienc
analysis analysi
Importance of Text Preprocessing
Text preprocessing is an essential step in Natural Language Processing because it:
Removes unnecessary words and symbols.
Reduces the size of the vocabulary.
Improves the efficiency and accuracy of NLP algorithms.
Standardizes text for better analysis.
Enhances keyword extraction and document classification.
