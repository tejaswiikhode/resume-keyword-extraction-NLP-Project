Introduction to Natural Language Processing(NLP)
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables
computers to understand, interpret, analyze, and generate human language. It combines
concepts from computer science, linguistics, and machine learning to process textual and
speech data efficiently. NLP allows computers to perform various tasks such as text
classification, sentiment analysis, machine translation, chatbot development, speech
recognition, and information extraction.
In today's digital world, large amounts of textual data are generated every second. NLP
helps organizations process this unstructured data and extract meaningful information from
it. It plays a significant role in improving human-computer interaction and automating
language-based tasks. NLP has applications across industries including healthcare, finance,
education, recruitment, e-commerce, and social media.
Applications of NLP
Some major applications of Natural Language Processing are:
1. Machine Translation – Converts text from one language to another automatically.
2. Text Classification – Categorizes textual data into predefined categories.
3. Sentiment Analysis – Determines whether a text expresses positive, negative, or neutral
sentiment.
4. Text Summarization – Produces concise summaries from lengthy documents.
5. Chatbots and Virtual Assistants – Enables intelligent conversational systems.
6. Spam Email Detection – Identifies unwanted or fraudulent emails.
7. Grammar and Spell Checking – Detects grammatical and spelling errors.
8. Question Answering Systems – Provides accurate responses to user queries.
9. Named Entity Recognition (NER) – Extracts entities such as names, dates, and
organizations from text.
10. Speech Recognition – Converts spoken language into text.
11. Information Extraction – Extracts useful information from unstructured text.
12. Resume Screening and Keyword Extraction – Automatically identifies important skills
and qualifications from resumes.

Selected Mini Project Topic
Resume Keyword Extraction using Natural LanguageProcessing (NLP)
About the Topic
Recruitment is one of the most important processes in every organization. Companies
receive hundreds or even thousands of resumes for a single job opening. Manually
reviewing every resume to identify relevant skills and qualifications is time-consuming and
inefficient.
The proposed project aims to develop a Resume Keyword Extraction System using Natural
Language Processing techniques. The system automatically analyzes resume text and
extracts important keywords such as technical skills, programming languages, educational
qualifications, certifications, and relevant experience. By identifying these keywords,
recruiters can quickly evaluate whether a candidate meets the requirements of a particular
job role.
The project involves preprocessing resume text by removing unnecessary characters,
converting text into a suitable format, tokenizing the content, and extracting meaningful
keywords using NLP techniques. Such systems are widely used in Applicant Tracking Systems
(ATS) to simplify and automate the recruitment process.
Aim
To study the applications of Natural Language Processing and formulate a problem
statement for developing a Resume Keyword Extraction System that automatically
identifies important keywords and skills from resumes.
Problem Statement
Recruiters often spend significant time reviewing resumes to identify suitable candidates for
a job position. Manual resume screening becomes increasingly difficult when dealing with a
large number of applications. Important skills or qualifications may sometimes be
overlooked, resulting in inefficient candidate selection.
The objective of this project is to develop a Natural Language Processing (NLP)-based
system that automatically extracts relevant keywords from resumes. The system will identify
information such as technical skills, programming languages, educational qualifications,
certifications, and work experience. By converting unstructured resume text into structured
information, the system can assist recruiters in shortlisting candidates more accurately and
efficiently. This approach reduces manual effort, saves time, and improves the overall
recruitment process.
