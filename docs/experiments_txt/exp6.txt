Theory
Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging is the process of assigning a grammatical category to each word in a sentence, such as noun, verb, adjective, adverb,
pronoun, or preposition. It helps identify the syntactic role of words and improves the understanding of text.
Different POS taggers include Rule-Based Taggers, Statistical Taggers, Transformation-Based Taggers, and the NLTK POS Tagger, which uses a
pretrained statistical model. In this experiment, the Resume Dataset is preprocessed, tokenized, and each word is assigned its corresponding POS tag
using NLTK.
POS tagging is widely used in information extraction, text summarization, machine translation, sentiment analysis, and question answering.

def perform_pos(text):
    text=text.lower()
    text=re.sub(r'[^a-z\s]',' ',text)
    tokens=word_tokenize(text)
    return pos_tag(tokens)
df["POS_Tags"]=df["Resume"].apply(perform_pos)
df[['Category','POS_Tags']].head()
df[['Category','Resume','POS_Tags']].to_csv(
